How it works

Three parts, one book. Collateral, debt and the AMM all sit in the same pool, which is why liquidation never needs an external buyer.

◆

Shared liquidity

Lenders and traders quote against the same depth. Borrowing demand lifts the rate instead of draining the book.

⚡

Self-clearing liquidations

When a position breaches LTV, the engine unwinds it into the pool it already borrows from. No keeper race, no discount auction.

◈

Isolated downside

Each pair carries its own debt ceiling and oracle set, so a single bad market cannot drag the rest of the protocol.

Liquidity

Supply stables or blue-chip collateral and earn the borrow side of the book. Rates are set by utilisation, paid per second.

No lockups

Withdraw whenever utilisation allows — no epoch queue, no vesting.

Backstop first

LPs are senior to trader PnL on the same pool, with a reserve funded from fees.

On-chain rates

Every rate change is a function of utilisation, verifiable in the contract.

Start in four steps

  1. Connect a wallet on the supported chain.
  2. Deposit collateral — ETH, wstETH, hBTC or stables.
  3. Choose leverage up to the market LTV, then open the position.
  4. Watch health in the position panel; top up or close any time.
